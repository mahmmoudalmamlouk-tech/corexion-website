Cookie Policy

How our website uses cookies and similar technologies, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

01What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website places on your device. Similar technologies, such as pixels and local storage, work in a comparable way. In this policy, "cookies" covers all of them.

02Cookies we use

We keep our use of cookies to a minimum. Our website does not use advertising cookies, and it does not track you across other websites.

  • Strictly necessary cookies: our hosting and security provider may set a small number of cookies to protect the website against malicious traffic and keep it available. These are essential to deliver the website you have asked to view, so they do not require your consent.

We do not currently use analytics cookies. If we introduce them in future, we will update this policy first and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before setting them.

03Third-party content

Our website loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When it does, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address. Google Fonts does not set cookies on our website.

04Managing cookies

Most browsers let you see, block and delete cookies through their settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of the website from working properly. Guidance for each browser is available at aboutcookies.org.

05More information

For details of how we handle personal information, please read our Privacy Policy. If you have any questions about cookies, contact us at info@corexion.uk.

06Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
