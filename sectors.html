Sectors we serve

Each sector has its own users, risks and delivery constraints. We start from what matters to the client in that sector, then apply the services that fit.

01

Investors, developers & funders

Funds, developers and lenders deciding whether and how to invest in a project or an asset.

CLIENT PRIORITIES
A clear, evidence-based basis for the investment decision
An independent view of cost, programme, risk and design
Confidence in the asset being bought, built or funded
02

Hospitality & resorts

Hotels, branded residences, leisure and guest environments.

CLIENT PRIORITIES
A fixed opening date and pre-opening programme
Operator brand standards and FF&E coordination
Quality of every guest-facing finish
03

Residential & mixed-use

Communities, towers and integrated developments.

CLIENT PRIORITIES
Phasing aligned with sales and handover dates
Interfaces between residential, retail and commercial uses
Quality and defects closed before handover
04

Commercial & workplace

Offices, corporate environments and commercial assets.

CLIENT PRIORITIES
Occupier requirements and a clear fit-out scope
Works planned around live operations
Whole-life cost and long-term flexibility
05

Retail & lifestyle

Retail, food and beverage, entertainment destinations.

CLIENT PRIORITIES
Tenant coordination and fit-out interfaces
Opening dates tied to trading
A consistent customer experience
06

Culture & education

Cultural, learning and community facilities.

CLIENT PRIORITIES
Stakeholder and user engagement
Works within operational buildings
Long-term value for the community
07

Infrastructure & public realm

Enabling works, utilities, landscape and external interfaces.

CLIENT PRIORITIES
Enabling works and utility diversions
Landscape and external interfaces
Safe public access during the works
