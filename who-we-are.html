ABOUT COREXION

Who we are & how we add value.

We connect design decisions with construction readiness, forecast cost and the value the client needs from the completed asset.

Project management, engineering review and design coordination within one integrated consultancy approach. Clients can appoint a focused service or combine disciplines around a common brief, clear responsibilities and one coordinated decision process.

We believe successful project management is measured by more than completion: by the achievement of your objectives, the benefits realised, and the value sustained beyond handover.
