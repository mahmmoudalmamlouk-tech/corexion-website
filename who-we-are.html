Who we are

Project management, engineering review and design coordination within one integrated consultancy approach.

A message from COREXION

Partnering for project success

This website goes beyond a presentation of COREXION's services. It sets out our strategic vision and a practical, evidence-based approach to managing projects - aligning investment objectives with delivery, translating ambition into structured plans, and guiding decisions through every stage of the project lifecycle.

At COREXION, we aim to be a trusted partner in your project success. We support your current projects by strengthening control, resolving delivery challenges, and improving performance. For your future projects, we help establish the strategy, priorities, and delivery foundations needed to move forward with confidence.

We invite you to explore these pages with your own organisation and projects in mind: the outcomes you seek, the challenges you face, and the opportunities to create lasting value. Our approach brings together scope, schedule, cost, quality, and risk within a coordinated management framework, supported by clear governance, accountable teams, and informed decision-making.

We believe that successful project management is measured by more than completion. It is measured by the achievement of your objectives, the benefits realised, and the value sustained beyond handover.

Integrated methodology

From client objectives to verified outcomes

One management cycle connects the investment purpose, delivery decisions and the value achieved.

01
Define objectives
Client brief, intended benefits, stakeholders and acceptance criteria.
Requirements & benefit baseline
02
Feasibility study
Site and technical constraints, outline cost and programme, key risks and the feasible options.
Options appraisal & recommended way forward
03
Business case
The need, options compared on net value and whole-life cost, affordability, delivery route and residual risks.
Decision-ready business case & benefit targets
04
Establish the plan
Scope, budget, programme, risk, procurement and decision authority.
Execution plan & control baselines
05
Coordinate delivery
Design releases, supply, workfront readiness and discipline interfaces.
Coordinated delivery information
06
Control performance
Verified progress, forecasts, quality, changes and corrective action.
Decision report & authorised actions
07
Verify outcomes
Acceptance, operational readiness, handover and measured benefits.
Closeout & benefit evidence
Evidence and lessons feed back into the plan. The client retains approval authority and ownership of operational benefits.
