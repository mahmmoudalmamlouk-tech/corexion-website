Investment advisory

From feasibility to realised benefits

We help clients decide whether to invest, how to invest, and whether the investment delivered what it promised, linking every stage to the same objectives, evidence and owners.

STAGE 01
Feasibility study
Test whether the project can meet its objectives before major commitment: site and technical constraints, outline cost and programme, key risks and the feasible options.
Output: Options appraisal and a recommended way forward.
STAGE 02
Business case
Set out the case for investment: the need, options compared on net value and whole-life cost, affordability, delivery route and residual risks.
Output: A decision-ready business case with benefit targets and named owners.
STAGE 03
Benefits realisation
Each benefit has an owner, a measurement plan and an acceptance requirement, tracked from baseline to measured outcome after handover.
Output: A benefits realisation plan and verified outcomes.
How benefits are tracked
01  Expected
Baseline, target and assumptions
02  Approved
Authorised target and owner
03  Implemented
Adopted solution and technical acceptance
04  Realised
Measured outcome and owner acceptance
Variance prompts corrective action and remeasurement. The client or operator owns realisation; approved targets are not guaranteed results.
ALSO WITHIN INVESTMENT ADVISORY
Due diligence report

An independent technical review for investors, lenders and buyers before they acquire, fund or commit to an asset or a project. We examine design, cost, programme, contracts, condition and risk, and set out what they mean for the investment decision.

Output: A due diligence report with findings, risks and recommendations.
