Privacy Policy

How COREXION LIMITED collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our website or contact us.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

01Who we are

This website is operated by COREXION LIMITED, a company registered in England and Wales ("COREXION", "we", "us").

COREXION is the controller of the personal information described in this policy. If you have any questions about it, please contact us at info@corexion.uk.

02The information we collect

We only collect the information we need to respond to you and to run our business:

  • Enquiry details you give us through the contact form or by email: your name, company, email address, phone number (optional), the service you are interested in and your message
  • Business contact details of clients, consultants, suppliers and their staff, where we work with them on a project or proposal
  • Basic technical information that any website receives when you visit it, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, processed by our hosting and security providers

We do not ask for sensitive information through this website, and we ask you not to send it to us.

03How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and arrange a conversation about your project
  • Prepare proposals and, where you appoint us, deliver and manage our services
  • Keep proper business, contract and accounting records
  • Keep our website secure and working properly
  • Meet our legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

04Our lawful basis

Under UK data protection law we must have a lawful basis for using personal information. We rely on:

  • Taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and performing a contract, when you ask us for a proposal or appoint us
  • Our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries, managing client relationships and keeping our website secure, where these are not overridden by your rights
  • Legal obligation, where we must keep records, for example for tax and accounting purposes
  • Consent, where we ask for it. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us

05Who we share it with

We share personal information only where it is needed, and only with:

  • Our own team in our offices, so the right people can respond to you
  • Service providers who host our website, provide our email and IT systems and keep the website secure, acting on our instructions
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, accountants and insurers, where necessary
  • Public authorities, where the law requires it

06International transfers

COREXION works with colleagues in the United Kingdom, Canada, the USA, Saudi Arabia, Egypt and Rwanda, and some of our service providers are based outside the UK. Where your information is transferred outside the UK, we make sure it is protected, for example by relying on UK adequacy regulations or by using the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

07How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it:

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an appointment: up to 24 months from our last contact
  • Client and project records: for the duration of the appointment and up to 6 years afterwards, or longer where a contract or the law requires it

After that, we delete or anonymise it securely.

08Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Ask us to delete your information, or to restrict how we use it
  • Object to our use of your information where we rely on legitimate interests
  • Ask for your information to be transferred to another organisation, where this applies
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, where we rely on consent

To use any of these rights, email info@corexion.uk. We will respond within one month.

09Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk.

10Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, and we limit access to those who need it.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.
